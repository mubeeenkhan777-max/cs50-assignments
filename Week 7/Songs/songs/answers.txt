If `songs.db` contains the top 100 songs of one listener from 2018, I would characterize their audio aura by looking at the average energy, valence, and danceability of their top 100 songs. A higher average energy and danceability could suggest that the listener prefers energetic and active music, while a higher valence could suggest that they tend to listen to more positive or cheerful music.

This calculation might not be very representative because it gives every song equal importance. For example, a song played once has the same effect on the average as a song played many times. A better approach would be to consider how frequently each song was played and how much time the listener spent listening to each song. Spotify could also consider the listener's most-played artists, genres, and changes in listening habits throughout the year.
